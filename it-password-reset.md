# Phishing Template: Urgent IT Password Reset

**Objective:** Social engineering the target into clicking a credential harvesting link.
**Target:** Corporate employees.
**Pretext:** A mandatory security update.

---

**Subject:** [ACTION REQUIRED] Mandatory Password Reset - IT Security

**From:** IT Support <it-support@company-portal-update.com> (Spoofed)

Dear Employee,

As part of our annual security maintenance, we are enforcing a mandatory password reset for all staff accounts by **Friday at 5:00 PM**. 

To maintain uninterrupted access to your email and internal systems, you must update your credentials immediately.

[ **CLICK HERE TO RESET YOUR PASSWORD** ] 

If you do not update your password, your account will be temporarily locked. 

Thank you for your cooperation.

*IT Service Desk*
